**The honest truth most founders don't want to hear:** Most early-stage SaaS founders are flying blind. They check Stripe daily, glance at user count, and call that "data-driven." Then they walk into a Series A meeting and a VC asks about Net Revenue Retention. Silence. That silence is expensive.

## The 7 KPIs at a glance

| KPI | What it measures | Healthy benchmark |
|-----|------------------|-------------------|
| **MRR** | Monthly recurring revenue | >15% MoM growth |
| **Churn Rate** | % customers lost per month | <2% monthly |
| **CAC** | Cost to acquire customer | Payback <12 months |
| **LTV** | Total revenue per customer | LTV:CAC >3:1 |
| **NRR** | Revenue retained + expanded | >100% |
| **Burn Rate** | Cash spent per month | 12+ months runway |
| **Activation Rate** | % reaching core value | >60% within 7 days |

## Why NRR is the hidden gem

NRR above 100% means your existing customers are spending more over time — your business grows even if you acquire zero new customers. This is the definition of a defensible SaaS business.

## What a Series A-ready dashboard looks like

- Single-screen summary of all 7 metrics
- Month-over-month trend lines
- Cohort retention tables
- One-click investor reports
